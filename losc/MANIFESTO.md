# Local Only Servers Club

## What We Are

Software that runs on your machine.
Not in a data center you don't control.
Not phoning home.
Not requiring an account to function.

Your compute. Your data. Your rules.

## The Rules

1. **It runs locally.** The primary function works without an internet connection.
2. **It does not phone home.** No telemetry, no analytics, no usage reporting. Not even opt-in by default.
3. **It declares its boundaries.** A manifest (SAFE-compatible or equivalent) that states what the software accesses, stores, and touches. Human-readable. Checked in to the repo.
4. **It survives your absence.** No subscription, no license server, no cloud dependency that can be turned off.
5. **Deletion is clean.** Remove the directory. It's gone. Nothing lingers in a cloud you never consented to.

## What We Are Not

We are not against the internet.
We are not against collaboration.
We are not against cloud services that you choose to use.

We are against software that requires the cloud to function,
that phones home by default,
that dies when someone else's server goes down.

## Membership

There is no application.
If your software follows the rules, you are in.
The bot will check. The bot is the first member.

## The Bot

The Willow GitHub bot enforces and celebrates these values.
When it sees a fork, it checks.
If the fork qualifies, it says so.
FRANK has logged it.
