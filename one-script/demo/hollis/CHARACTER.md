# Hollis Varga

Hollis Varga is 54. He has been an independent piano tuner and technician for about thirty years and works as
**Varga Piano Service** from his house at 18 Cobble Lane, Larkfield, a fictional small town. He drives a white 2014
Transit Connect van to around 200 private clients a year across the towns near Larkfield (Coldbrook, Harmon Ridge,
Pine Hatch, Ashby Mills, Wrenhaven, Saltmarsh). He also has standing accounts with a church, a school district, a
music academy, a community theatre, the Civic Hall (a concert Steinway D) and a bar in Coldbrook. He tunes by ear and
is proud of it.

**Family.**
- **Imre**, his father, is 81, a widower, and lives alone in Brookhollow, 38 miles away. Hollis visits on Sundays.
- **Klara**, his mother, died in 2020. Her 1911 Weiler & Sons upright has been in Hollis's garage since 2021. He has
  been restoring it, on and off.
- **Corinne**, his ex-wife (now Corinne Haldane), remarried. Their divorce and the house buy-out ended in early 2022.
- **Nell** (Eleanor), their daughter, is 25. She lives in Rivermouth, about three hours away. She finished an
  occupational-therapy master's in May 2026 and started her first job.
- **Ruth Tamsin** lives next door to Imre and keeps an eye on him.

**Others in his files.** Dana Ostrowski is his accountant. Marta Quill runs events at the Civic Hall. Gil Marchetti
is a theatre volunteer. Dr. Lindqvist is his GP. Dunmore Auto & Diesel is his garage, and Meridian Piano Supply is a
parts supplier.

## How his life writes itself down

He is not organised so much as habitual. The folder is `life/`, about 395 files and 0.5 MB, covering March 2021 to
October 2026.

| Folder | What | Approx. count |
|---|---|---|
| `work/tuning_log_YYYY.csv` | Every visit: client, town, instrument, pitch found, work, minutes, fee, how paid, callback, a note | 6 |
| `work/invoices/` | Text invoices for the institutional accounts | ~115 |
| `work/client_notes.txt` | One file of client quirks | 1 |
| `mail/inbox`, `mail/sent` | Emails saved as `.eml`: accountant, venues, family, suppliers, guild bulletins, promos, inquiries, junk | ~110 |
| `messages/` | Phone text exports, one file per year each for Nell and Dad | 12 |
| `money/bank/` | Monthly checking-account CSV exports, 2022 onward | 58 |
| `money/taxes/` | His own quarterly-estimate ledger per year, plus typed-up letters | 7 |
| `money/house/` | Leftover papers from the divorce buy-out | 2 |
| `receipts/` | Receipts typed or saved as text | ~38 |
| `notes/` | Dated scraps, a recipe and todo lists | ~45 |
| `van/` | A daily odometer log he keeps for the accountant, and the garage's printed service history | 2 |
| `weiler/` | The restoration log for his mother's piano and a garage hygrometer export | 2 |
| `calendar/hollis_calendar.ics` | A calendar export | 1 |

Today, in his world, is **2026-10-22**.

## What he'd say he wants from a tool

> "I don't remember what I called anything or where I put it. I want to type what's on my mind, the way I'd say
> it to someone, and have it show me the thing. And if there's something I've let slide, I'd rather it showed me than
> wait for me to ask."
