## What the misses have in common

Every miss is a kind of reading, not a missing word or a missing file:

1. **Her word isn't the file's word.** due / next (N1); worn out / "fine" (N7).
2. **Newest first.** The bees, Harriet's last tuning, Dad: every answer is
   the latest rows, and every serve started from 2020 or 2021 (N5, N1).
3. **Something that isn't there.** A PO with no deposit (N4); a 2026 notice
   with no reply (N3, found only because its text said "need").
4. **Numbers over time.** 71.2 kg to 75.1 kg is no word at all (N2).

Four readings. A list of what she needs reduces to them, so they're what to
keep. Everything else in a bigger tool is what to take away.
