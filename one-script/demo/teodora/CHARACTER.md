# Teodora "Teo" Marinescu

33, born in Cluj, Romania; in Leeds, UK since 2015. Self-employed bookkeeper
for a handful of small local businesses (a plumber, a café, a florist, a dog
groomer; an electrician until mid-2025). Works from the kitchen table of a
rented two-bed flat.

Lives with her partner **Sam Okafor** (secondary-school teacher) and, on
Wednesdays, Thursdays and alternate weekends, Sam's daughter **Maisie** (8,
Year 4). Maisie's mum is **Jo Whitlam**; the co-parenting is friendly and
logistical. Teo's sister **Ana** lives in Cluj and is engaged to **Radu**.
Their grandmother **Floarea** ("bunica") died in January 2022 and left four
cassette-style voice recordings of herself talking through recipes. Close
friend **Dev Raman** (pub quiz, lent money when the boiler died) and his
partner Priya are expecting their first baby.

Sam's mum is **Grace**. Clients: Gary and Lynn at Hargreaves Plumbing, Kirsty
at Bramble & Bean, Hannah at Petal Row Flowers, Mel at Fernside Dog Grooming,
Chidi at Okonjo Electrical (left June 2025).

She is halfway through a professional qualification (AAT Level 4) she started
in 2023 and stopped working on in 2024. She has a sore right wrist that
she half-treats. She is good at other people's paperwork and patchy at her own.
